overview: >-
  Engineered the Invoice & Warehouse Management System (IWMS) for the Ducati project to eliminate redundancies and minimize human errors caused by manual data entry processes between external consignment warehouses and the organization's internal accounting system. The system was architected to serve as a robust Middleware, seamlessly bridging data from the Oracle ERP system into a centralized application database. This integration automatically facilitates the generation of commercial documents and tracks inventory movement in real-time. Furthermore, it features an interactive Executive Dashboard that enables management to monitor inventory valuation and perform detailed Sunk Cost analysis on the fly. Ultimately, the IWMS completely resolves the data silos between the Sales and Accounting departments, streamlining the entire consignment process.
features:
  - "Centralized Middleware Integration: Serves as a real-time data hub between Oracle ERP and external warehouses, eliminating data silos between Sales and Accounting in the consignment process."
  - "Executive Dashboard & Inventory Valuation: Interactive real-time dashboard featuring automated inventory valuation and Top 10 Sunk Cost analysis for data-driven management decisions."
  - "Smart Data Synchronization & Pre-check Stock: Intelligent sync engine with strict pre-transaction stock validation to absolutely prevent negative inventory anomalies."
  - "Approval Workflow Management: Structured approval routing for critical commercial documents to ensure all stock movements are verified by authorized personnel."
  - "Automated Reconciliation & Reporting Module: Auto-reconciliation engine coupled with comprehensive reporting for Stock On Hand, Stock Cards, Invoice Movement, and All Transactions."
  - "Excel Export & Financial Document Support: Automated Excel exports to reduce manual workloads, assisting Accounting in calculating precise price variances for Debit/Credit Notes."
challenges:
  - problem: "Severe data silos and redundancies between Sales and Accounting departments due to manual data entry from Oracle ERP and external warehouses."
    solution: "Developed a robust Middleware application with a Smart Data Synchronization engine to automatically fetch and update records, ensuring a single source of truth in real-time."
  - problem: "High risk of negative inventory and race conditions when multiple users attempt to deduct stock concurrently."
    solution: "Implemented Pessimistic Locking and strict Database Transactions coupled with pre-transaction validations (Pre-check) to lock records and guarantee data integrity during concurrent operations."
  - problem: "Manual inventory valuation, sunk cost analysis, and account reconciliation were incredibly time-consuming and error-prone."
    solution: "Built a real-time Executive Dashboard that automatically evaluates inventory and ranks sunk costs, integrated with an Automated Reconciliation module and Excel export functionalities to eliminate manual efforts."
